Article builder

Lay out a project write-up from your photos and videos, then export it as a static web page.

  • Pick the folder that holds the project's media. Subfolders are included.
  • Your layout saves to project.json in that folder as you work.
  • Export writes the finished page to an export folder inside it.

This needs Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer, because it reads and writes files in a folder you choose.
